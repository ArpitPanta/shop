Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can you deliver?

We offer same-day delivery in Kathmandu for orders placed before 2 PM. Standard delivery takes 24 hours for other areas.

Can I customize my bouquet?

Absolutely! We can customize any arrangement to your preferences. Contact us directly with your requirements.

What is your refund policy?

We offer 100% refund if you're not satisfied with the quality. Just let us know within 24 hours of delivery.

Do you offer corporate orders?

Yes! We handle bulk orders for offices, events, and special occasions with competitive pricing.

How long do the flowers last?

Our fresh flowers typically last 7-10 days with proper care. We include care instructions with every delivery.

Can I send flowers internationally?

Currently we deliver within Nepal. For international orders, please contact us for special arrangements.
