About The Fresh Flower Mala Shop

Our Story

The Fresh Flower Mala Shop was founded with a simple mission: to bring happiness through beautiful, fresh flower malai. For over a decade, we have been serving customers across Nepal with handcrafted flower malai that express feelings and blessings when words fall short.

Our Mission

We believe that flowers have the power to convey emotions, bless moments, and create lasting memories. Our mission is to make quality flower malai accessible to everyone and deliver arrangements that not only look beautiful but also last long and bring joy and blessings to the recipient.

Why Choose Us?

  • ✓ Expert florists with years of malai-stringing experience
  • ✓ Same-day delivery available in Kathmandu
  • ✓ Customizable malai for any occasion and ceremony
  • ✓ Three premium categories: Rose Mala, Tube Rose Mala, and Mix Mala
  • ✓ Competitive pricing with premium quality
  • ✓ 100% satisfaction guarantee

Our Team

Our team consists of passionate florists, designers, and customer service professionals dedicated to making your experience special. We take pride in every arrangement we create and stand behind our work with a satisfaction guarantee.
